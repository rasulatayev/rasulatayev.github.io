Ask me anything

A conversation
before we meet.

Some questions about how I work, what motivates me and what I am looking for next.

Prepared answers, not an AI chatbot.

What is it like working with you?

I try to be helpful and dependable. If I don't know how to do something, I usually look for a way to learn it and prepare myself.

How do you approach unfamiliar tasks?

I first try to understand the problem myself. Then I research, use available tools and learn as I work. I enjoy that process.

How do you handle disagreement?

I explain the reasons behind my suggestion. If my manager decides on a different approach after considering it, I respect that decision.

What is something you are working on?

I sometimes invest more time than necessary in finding a better solution. I want to get better at balancing the effort with what the team actually needs.

What motivates you?

Curiosity, being useful to someone, personal growth and learning. Ultimately, I want to become someone I can be proud of.

What are you looking for in your next role?

An international environment where I can contribute, learn from experienced people, take on new challenges and gradually earn greater responsibility.

What do you enjoy outside work?

Playing guitar, listening to music, cooking, travelling and spending time with animals. I especially enjoy listening closely to how music is put together. I also feed stray cats. One bite and a course of rabies vaccinations later, I’m still feeding them.

Why did you create this website?

I wanted to introduce myself in a more personal way than a CV alone allows. Building it was also an opportunity to learn and experiment.
